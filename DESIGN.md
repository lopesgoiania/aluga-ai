# Aluga Mais — Aurora Green Glass

## Direction

Aluga Mais is an operational workspace for real estate teams. The interface uses a calm, luminous glass language inspired by the Dashboard-Replica reference: a soft neutral canvas, translucent white work surfaces, a black active state, and a neon-green operational accent. The visual tone is precise and trustworthy rather than decorative.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#f7f7f7` fading into a soft gray background.
- Ink: `#0b1320`; muted text: `#7a7a7a`.
- Accent: `#76fb91`; pressed accent: `#4fd96b`; active navigation: `#0b0f14`.
- Surface: `rgba(255,255,255,.80)`; strong surface: `rgba(255,255,255,.92)`.
- Border: `rgba(11,19,32,.11)`.
- Radius: 12px controls, 15–20px work surfaces, pill only for statuses and filters.
- Shadow: `0 18px 50px rgba(42,76,111,.11)` plus a small lift shadow.

## Interaction rules

- Keep the navigation rail dark and stable; the workspace is light and airy.
- Use blue for primary action, teal for healthy/connected states, amber for review, and red only for destructive or failed states.
- Preserve the WhatsApp-like conversation model inside the shared glass shell.
- Every focusable control needs a visible focus ring and every mobile route must remain reachable through the bottom navigation or “Mais”.
- Prefer one strong surface per section over nested cards. Use borders and spacing to create hierarchy.
